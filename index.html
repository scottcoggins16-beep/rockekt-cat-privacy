Rocket Cat Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

Rocket Cat is a mobile game developed by Scott Coggins. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Rocket Cat.

Information We Collect

Rocket Cat does not require you to create an account or provide your real name, email address, phone number, or physical address to play the game.

The game creates a random, anonymous player identifier on your device. This identifier is used to associate your scores with your leaderboard entry.

Leaderboard Information

If you use the online leaderboard, Rocket Cat may send your anonymous player identifier, your in-game player name, and your score to our leaderboard service. The leaderboard may also store the date and time associated with a submitted score.

The default in-game player name is "ROCKET CAT." Rocket Cat does not require you to enter personally identifying information for leaderboard participation.

Third-Party Services

Rocket Cat uses Supabase to provide online leaderboard functionality. Information sent to the leaderboard service is handled according to Supabase's applicable privacy practices.

Advertising

Rocket Cat does not currently use third-party advertising services.

Children's Privacy

Rocket Cat does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game does not require users to provide personal information to play.

Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information associated with the game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Rocket Cat's data practices, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Google Play store listing.
